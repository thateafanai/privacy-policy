BCM Missionary Report Application

Data Deletion Policy

How to remove your data from this app and our cloud services.

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

We respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. Most data stays on your device, with an optional encrypted cloud backup if you choose to sign in with Google. This page explains what is stored and how to delete it.

How Your Data is Stored

  • Local storage: All reports, activities, and profile information are stored on your device in a local SQLite database.
  • Cloud backup (optional): If you sign in with Google, your data is also securely backed up to Google Firebase, encrypted in transit and accessible only to your account.
  • Missionary directory (optional): If you sign in with Google, a limited set of fields — name, category/position, field, region, church, joined year, and phone number — is shared with other signed-in users via the in-app "BCM Missionaries" directory.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Clear All Data from Within the App

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap "Clear All Data".
  3. The app automatically saves a local backup before deleting anything.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm the permanent deletion.

This permanently removes all your reports, activities, and profile information from your device.

If you are signed in with Google, this also deletes your data from our Firebase cloud storage and removes your entry from the "BCM Missionaries" directory.

Option 2: Uninstall the App

Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored locally on your device. If you had previously signed in with Google and used Cloud Sync, your encrypted cloud backup and directory entry remain on our servers until removed — use Option 1 first, or contact us (below) to request their removal.

Option 3: Unlink Your Google Account

Go to Settings → Unlink Google to disconnect your Google account and turn off Cloud Sync. This stops future syncing but does not delete data already stored in the cloud — use "Clear All Data" beforehand, or contact us, to remove it.

Data Retention

  • Local data is retained only on your device and is removed when you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud backups exist only for accounts that have signed in with Google, and are deleted when you use "Clear All Data" or request removal.
  • We do not keep separate backups of your data on our servers beyond your own Firebase-synced copy.

Your Rights

  • Access your data at any time within the app.
  • Export your reports as PDF files.
  • Delete your local and cloud data permanently via "Clear All Data."
  • Request removal of any remaining cloud or directory data by contacting us.
