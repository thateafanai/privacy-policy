BCM Missionary Report Application

Privacy Policy

How we collect, store, and protect your information.

Last updated: June 2026

Purpose of This App

This application is exclusively designed for Baptist Church of Mizoram (BCM) missionaries to submit their monthly ministry reports digitally.

Data Collection

We collect only ministry-related information necessary for monthly reporting:

  • Personal identification (name, position, location).
  • Ministry activities and statistics.
  • Contact information for official correspondence.
  • Google Account information (email, user ID) for authentication and sync, if you sign in with Google.

Data Storage

  • Local Storage: All data is primarily stored on your device using a secure local database.
  • Cloud Backup: If signed in with Google, your data is securely backed up to Google Firebase with encryption.
  • Data remains under your control and can be deleted anytime.

Data Usage

Your data is used solely for:

  • Generating monthly ministry reports.
  • Tracking ministry activities and progress.
  • Official BCM reporting requirements.
  • Enabling cross-device synchronization via Google Firebase, if signed in with Google.

Data Sharing

We do NOT:

  • Sell or share your data with third parties.
  • Use your data for marketing purposes.
  • Access your data without your consent.

When using Google Sign-In, minimal data (user ID, email) is shared with Google Firebase for authentication and sync purposes only.

In-App Missionary Directory NEW

When you sign in with Google, your name, category/position, field, region, church, joined year, and phone number are shared with other signed-in BCM missionaries through the in-app "BCM Missionaries" directory, so they can contact you for ministry coordination (e.g. Call/WhatsApp).

This directory is visible only to other signed-in users of this app — not the public. If you do not sign in with Google, you are not listed.

Cloud Sync

When signed in with Google:

  • Data syncs automatically when internet is available.
  • Encrypted transmission to secure Firebase servers.
  • Access limited to your account only.
  • You can disable cloud sync at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all your stored data.
  • Export your reports in various formats.
  • Delete your data permanently.
  • Opt-out of cloud sync (use local storage only).

Security

We protect your data through:

  • Device-level security (PIN/password/biometric).
  • Secure Google authentication with OAuth 2.0.
  • Encrypted cloud storage using Firebase security rules.
  • No unauthorized access to your reports.

Crash & Diagnostic Data NEW

To find and fix problems and improve reliability, the app sends crash and diagnostic data through Google Firebase Crashlytics — in the released app only, not during development.

  • Error details and stack traces (what went wrong, and where in the code).
  • Device model, operating-system version, and app version.
  • If you are signed in, an anonymous account identifier so a crash can be traced to an account.

We never collect your name, email, phone number, reports, or activity content for this purpose. This data is processed by Google Firebase; Google’s privacy policy applies.

Google Services

When using Google Sign-In:

  • We use Google Firebase for authentication and cloud storage.
  • Google’s privacy policy applies for authentication data: policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can revoke Google access through your Google Account settings.
